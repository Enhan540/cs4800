Privacy Policy

Group Planner helps groups find meeting times and manage invitations. This page explains the information the app uses.

Account information

When you sign in with Google, Group Planner receives your Google account identifier, verified email address, and name. We use these to create and secure your account and show your identity in the app. Google sign-in does not grant calendar access.

Calendar information

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Group Planner lists calendars you can access and lets you choose which calendars contribute free and busy times. We store encrypted authorization credentials, selected calendar identifiers and names, and calculated busy time intervals for scheduling. We do not request event titles, descriptions, or guests, and do not change your calendars. Other group members see availability results, not your calendar names or event details.

If you add an iCal subscription, Group Planner receives the feed to calculate busy times. The feed may contain event titles and other details while we process it. We encrypt the subscription URL and store only normalized busy time intervals, not event details.

Other information

Group Planner stores the profile, group, availability, plan, invitation, and RSVP information you provide. It uses this information to run the scheduling service and display it to the relevant group members.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google sign-in when another sign-in method is available, and disconnect a connected calendar in Account settings. Disconnecting a calendar removes its stored connection and cached busy times. You can also revoke Group Planner's access in your Google Account permissions or reset an iCal secret address in its calendar provider.
